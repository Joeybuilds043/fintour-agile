# Destination Content Research Template

Use one completed copy per destination.

## Identity
Canonical name; province/region; administrative coverage; cluster anchor coordinates; local contributors and reviewers.

## Visitor proposition
What makes the destination distinct? Which visitor segments are relevant? Which experience categories are supported by evidence?

## Attractions and experiences
Name; type; coordinates; description; managing authority; opening status; access conditions; fees; source; last verified date.

## Culture and heritage
Languages and local identity; historical context; food; craft; festivals; religious and sacred sites; visitor etiquette; consent and cultural sensitivity notes; local reviewer.

## Practical information
Transport modes; route sources; seasonal access; accommodation options; accessibility; emergency and safety references; permits; last verified date.

## Environment and community
Protected-area status; waste and water issues; ecological sensitivities; community participation; local business visibility; visitor pressure.

## Data and media
Image source; creator; licence; attribution; data sources; publication date; observation period; confidence label.

## Review status
Draft; desk-researched; locally reviewed; authority checked; published; needs re-verification.
