# Prototype Evaluation Plan

## Visitor tasks
Find a destination matching an experience; open a guide and identify practical travel checks; find cultural and responsible-travel information; create a draft itinerary; interpret weather data and its limitations.

## Industry tasks
Identify available and missing destination indicators; distinguish official data from assumptions; enter investment assumptions and interpret break-even occupancy; identify risks excluded from the simplified model.

## Measures
Task completion rate; time to find relevant information; user-reported clarity and trust; share of key facts with traceable sources; unsupported claims found in review; financial calculator test coverage; mobile usability observations.

## Pilot approach
Run small, clearly described usability sessions with domestic visitors, hospitality students and tourism businesses. Report sample size and limitations. Small prototype tests do not establish national economic impact.
