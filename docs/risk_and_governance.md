# Risk, Safety, Culture and Data Governance

## Travel and safety
- Never label a route open or safe without a current authoritative source.
- Forecast weather is not a substitute for road, flood, avalanche or mountain-safety advice.
- Do not publish emergency contact details without checking the relevant jurisdiction and update date.
- Clearly mark access, permit, price and opening-hour information that requires confirmation.

## Cultural respect
- Seek community input for cultural descriptions and experience design.
- Obtain consent before photographing people, ceremonies or private spaces.
- Do not commodify sacred or sensitive cultural practices.
- Make local benefit-sharing and fair compensation explicit in proposed business models.

## Data quality
- Store source, observation period, unit, geography, retrieval date and limitations.
- Distinguish official, verified partner, survey-based, estimated, illustrative and missing data.
- Do not use estimated occupancy or spending as if observed.
- Keep an audit trail when records are edited or verified.

## Privacy
- Collect the minimum personal information needed.
- Do not expose private business data without permission.
- Define consent, retention and deletion practices before user accounts or business submissions are introduced.

## AI
- Ground answers in the platform catalogue and sourced records.
- Require AI to disclose missing information and assumptions.
- Do not rely on AI for safety, legal, medical, regulatory or investment approval decisions.
