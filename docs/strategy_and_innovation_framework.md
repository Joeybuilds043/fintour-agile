# Strategy and Innovation Framework

Horizon applies business strategy and innovation management to a real tourism-sector problem.

| Academic theme | Application | Evidence to develop |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic management | Destination positioning, competitiveness and portfolio prioritisation | Destination strategy profiles |
| Service innovation | Redesign discovery, planning and visitor information | Visitor journeys and usability tests |
| Innovation and technology transfer | Integrate mapping, open data, AI and digital service design | Architecture and working integrations |
| Sustainable and responsible innovation | Balance visitor value with ecological limits and community benefits | Sustainability and local participation measures |
| Digital business models | Connect visitors, local suppliers and destination organisations | Partner model and value proposition |
| Entrepreneurship and finance | Assess tourism business concepts | Feasibility and scenario models |

## Proposed development question
How can integrated digital discovery, destination data and decision-support tools improve visitor experiences, destination competitiveness and local economic participation in Pakistan?

## Impact logic
Inputs: destination information, verified sources, local knowledge, open technology and research.
Activities: discovery, trip planning, data integration, investment analysis and user testing.
Outputs: structured destination information, accessible itineraries, transparent evidence and business scenarios.
Intended outcomes: improved discoverability, stronger local enterprise visibility, more informed decisions and better destination management.
Long-term impact is a hypothesis requiring partnerships, evaluation and sustained implementation.

Horizon is an early-stage prototype. Do not claim national impact, validated demand, formal partnerships or proven economic outcomes without evidence.
