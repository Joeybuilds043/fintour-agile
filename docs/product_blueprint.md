# Horizon Tourism — Product Blueprint

## Proposition
A Pakistan-focused digital destination discovery and tourism intelligence prototype connecting visitor travel planning with industry decision support.

## Problems
Visitors face fragmented destination information and inconsistent access to practical, culturally informed travel guidance. Tourism businesses and destination managers often lack comparable evidence on demand, seasonality, accommodation, transport, visitor spending and local economic retention.

## Users
Domestic and international visitors; local tourism businesses and community enterprises; destination managers; entrepreneurs and investors; researchers and policy stakeholders.

## Workspaces
Discover Pakistan; destination guides; trip planner; explore map; destination intelligence; investment studio; data observatory.

## Principles
Do not fabricate live information, statistics, prices, hotel availability or safety status. Every image exposes source, creator and individual licence. Every statistic carries source, observation period, unit and geography. Distinguish official, verified-partner, survey-based, estimated, illustrative and missing data. Represent local communities respectfully and consider consent and benefit-sharing.

## Pilot scope
Ten clusters are a curated pilot, not a ranking: Hunza, Skardu, Swat, Chitral/Kalash, Neelum, Lahore, Taxila, Karachi/Makli–Thatta, Bahawalpur/Cholistan, Hingol/Gwadar.

## Out of scope initially
Booking/payment processing; nationwide verified hotel occupancy; real-time road safety or emergency dispatch; unmoderated reviews; national forecasts without validated baseline data.
